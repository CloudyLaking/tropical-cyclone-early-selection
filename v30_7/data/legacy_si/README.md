# Frozen figure data

The files contain saved summaries, storm-level results, member-rank histories, and two trajectory examples used in the manuscript. The runner verifies their SHA-256 checksums before drawing. No raw archive download is required.

## Files

| Files | Contents |
|---|---|
| `performance_summary.csv` | Saved track errors and paired gains by lead and cohort |
| `count_curves.csv`, `count_minima.csv` | Retained-fraction curves and saved retrospective sample minima |
| `fixed_summary.csv`, `fixed_storms.csv` | Fixed-list summaries and storm-level along-track, cross-track and distance errors |
| `rank_806_summary.csv`, `rank_817_summary.csv` | Rank/group statistics on two explicitly distinct cohorts |
| `member_histories.csv.gz` | Member identifiers and rank histories used in the ranking figures |
| `geometry_cases.csv`, `geometry_summary.csv`, `storm_geometry_summary.csv` | Selection-displacement geometry at initialization, storm and summary levels |
| `illustrative_track.npz`, `illustrative_track_ncep.npz` | ECMWF/NCEP forecasts of Typhoon Hagibis (2019), initialized at 12 UTC on 7 October 2019 |
| `case_provenance.json` | Storm identity, fixed case rule and member counts for those two examples |
| `fraction_summary.csv` | Signed errors by retained fraction |
| `coverage_summary.csv`, `coverage_crossings.csv` | Member ranges, empirical coverage, paired contrasts and boundary crossings |
| `spatial120_grid.csv` | Saved gridded 120-hour signed errors for Figure S15 |

The NPZ examples contain numeric/Unicode arrays and are read with `allow_pickle=False`. `p` stores aligned member error vectors, `truth_xy` the verifying local path, `ids` member identifiers, and `selected` the saved early selection. The plotting code constructs member positions from `p + truth_xy[:, None, :]`.

## Conventions

Distances and signed errors are in kilometres. ATE is along-track error, positive along the preceding observed 12-hour motion; CTE is cross-track error, positive to its right. `sid` identifies a storm and `base` an initialization. Multiple initializations can belong to one storm.

The 806-case complete-motion cohort contains 300 ECMWF initializations from 64 storms and 506 NCEP initializations from 100 storms. The original 817-case target cohort and the fixed-168-hour cohort are separate samples; they must not be pooled. Aggregated results weight storms equally. Proportions are converted to percentages explicitly in the plotting scripts where required.

Intervals are saved pointwise whole-storm resampling intervals, conditional on the original memberships and predictions. Hindsight lists use future verification, and stored best-found results do not certify a global optimum. No new statistics or member selection are generated by the figure runner.

## Original sources and transformations

These research-derived summaries and illustrative subsets were produced from western North Pacific TIGGE tropical-cyclone track forecasts and IBTrACS v04r01 verification for 2015–2024:

- **TIGGE model tropical cyclone track data**, NSF NCAR GDEX d330003: [dataset record](https://gdex.ucar.edu/datasets/d330003/), [doi:10.5065/D6GH9GSZ](https://doi.org/10.5065/D6GH9GSZ).
- **IBTrACS v04r01**, NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information: [dataset](https://doi.org/10.25921/82ty-9e16); Knapp et al. (2010), [The International Best Track Archive for Climate Stewardship](https://doi.org/10.1175/2009BAMS2755.1). Verification uses the western North Pacific main-track USA position fields.

Forecasts were matched to best tracks and transformed into the errors, ranks, group summaries and local projected coordinates used here. Memberwise 24-hour alignment removes the saved anchor-position error. The two NPZ files are transformed illustrative subsets, not unmodified original forecast files.
